WHAT COMES NEXT?

Future observatories and explorers will test today's ideas and reveal questions we have not learned to ask yet.

Upcoming milestones and long-range goals

2027 · Artemis III

Planned crewed demonstration in low Earth orbit to test key operations with commercial human landing system test vehicles.

2027 · NEO Surveyor target

NASA lists a 2027 target for its infrared asteroid-hunting observatory. Launch timing remains subject to mission readiness.

2028 · Dragonfly launch target

Dragonfly is planned to launch toward Titan, where it will investigate surface chemistry and habitability-related questions.

Early 2028 · Artemis IV target

NASA currently targets this mission for a crewed lunar landing, following Artemis III systems demonstrations.

Long term · Moon to Mars

Human exploration concepts include sustained lunar operations, improved life-support systems, radiation protection, and eventual crewed Mars missions. These are long-range goals, not fixed launch dates.

Important distinction

“Planned,” “targeted,” “under development,” and “proposed concept” mean different things. A concept such as interstellar laser-sail travel is not equivalent to a funded spacecraft with a scheduled launch.

For current official schedules, consult the NASA launch schedule.
