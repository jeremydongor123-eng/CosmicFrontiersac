NASA Artemis III

NASA currently describes Artemis III as a crewed low-Earth-orbit demonstration to test rendezvous and docking with commercial lunar lander systems before a planned 2028 landing mission.

NASA Artemis IV

NASA is targeting Artemis IV for the first crewed lunar landing in this updated sequence, with the aim of exploring near the lunar South Pole.
