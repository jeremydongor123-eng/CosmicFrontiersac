Explore the universe

Planets

Worlds orbiting stars. Our solar system has eight recognized planets, from rocky Mercury to ice giant Neptune.

Galaxies

Vast gravitational systems containing stars, gas, dust, stellar remnants, and dark matter.

Black Holes

Regions whose event horizons mark the boundary beyond which escape is impossible.

Nebulae

Interstellar clouds of gas and dust. Some are stellar nurseries; others are remnants of dying stars.

Exoplanets

Planets beyond our solar system, detected using methods such as transits and radial velocity.

Neutron Stars

Ultra-dense stellar remnants. Some rotate rapidly and emit beams observed as pulsars.
